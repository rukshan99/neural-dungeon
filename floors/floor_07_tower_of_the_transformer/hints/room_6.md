A mean over the whole batch is the mean of the shard means only when every shard is the same size, so `shard_batch` refuses anything that does not divide: `n = x.shape[0] // world_size` and rank r takes `x[r * n:(r + 1) * n]` (same slice for `y`). `average_gradients` is `torch.stack([g[name] for g in grad_dicts]).mean(0)` per name; the weighted version multiplies each dict by its size and divides by the total. In the simulation, ONE model, and `model.zero_grad(set_to_none=True)` before every rank's `loss.backward()`.
---
Inside `worker`: `torch.set_num_threads(1)`; `dist.init_process_group("gloo", init_method=f"file://{init_file}", rank=rank, world_size=world_size, timeout=timedelta(seconds=120))`; load `x`, `y` with `torch.load`, shard them, `model = build_foundry_model(); model.train()`, forward, `loss.backward()`. Then per parameter: `g = p.grad.detach().clone(); dist.all_reduce(g, op=dist.ReduceOp.SUM); reduced[name] = g / world_size`. Rank 0 does `torch.save(reduced, os.path.join(out_dir, REDUCED_GRADS_FILE))`; everyone ends with `dist.destroy_process_group()`. `ddp_worker` is the same up to the model, then `ddp = nn.parallel.DistributedDataParallel(model)`, forward through `ddp`, `loss.backward()`, and save `p.grad` over `ddp.module.named_parameters()`; no all_reduce of your own.
---
`launch_data_parallel`: `tmp = Path(tmp_dir); tmp.mkdir(parents=True, exist_ok=True)`; `torch.save(x, tmp / "x.pt")` and the same for `y`; `init_file = tmp / "rendezvous"; init_file.unlink(missing_ok=True)`; `fn = ddp_worker if use_ddp else worker`; `ctx = mp.spawn(fn, args=(world_size, str(init_file), str(tmp / "x.pt"), str(tmp / "y.pt"), str(tmp)), nprocs=world_size, join=False)`; then `while not ctx.join(timeout=remaining): if past the deadline, kill ctx.processes and raise TimeoutError`; finally `return torch.load(tmp / REDUCED_GRADS_FILE)`. If the trial reports the gradient is 2x too large, you forgot `/ world_size` after the SUM; if the DDP trial complains about `module.` names, you read `ddp.named_parameters()` instead of `ddp.module.named_parameters()`.
