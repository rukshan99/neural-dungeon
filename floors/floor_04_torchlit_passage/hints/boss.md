Phase 1 is three calls: `random.seed(seed)`, `np.random.seed(seed)`, `torch.manual_seed(seed)`, plus `torch.use_deterministic_algorithms(deterministic)` so the flag is set either way. Phase 2 is `build_run` followed by a list comprehension over `run_epoch`. The bitwise test passes as soon as the model is built AFTER seeding and the DataLoader gets `generator=torch.Generator().manual_seed(seed)`.
---
Phase 3, the checkpoint: `torch.save({"model": model.state_dict(), "optimizer": optimizer.state_dict(), "epoch": epoch, "rng_state": rng_state}, path)` and `torch.load(path, map_location="cpu", weights_only=True)`. The `rng_state` you pass in `train_with_resume` is `{"torch": torch.get_rng_state(), "loader": loader.generator.get_state()}`: both are plain tensors, so the safe loader accepts them.
---
Phase 3, the resume: after `build_run(seed)` a second time, restore four things from the checkpoint: `model.load_state_dict(ck["model"])`, `optimizer.load_state_dict(ck["optimizer"])`, `torch.set_rng_state(ck["rng_state"]["torch"])`, `loader.generator.set_state(ck["rng_state"]["loader"])`. Then `for _ in range(ck["epoch"], epochs): losses.append(run_epoch(...))`. Skip any one of the four and the third epoch drifts: weights (obvious), momentum (small drift), dropout masks (torch RNG), shuffle order (generator).
