`unbroadcast` is two small loops. First sum away leading axes that broadcasting inserted: `while grad.ndim > len(shape): grad = grad.sum(axis=0)`. Then sum over axes that were stretched from 1: `for i, d in enumerate(shape): if d == 1 and grad.shape[i] != 1: grad = grad.sum(axis=i, keepdims=True)`. Every binary op's backward ends with `self.grad += unbroadcast(<local gradient>, self.shape)` and the same for `other`. Unary ops (exp, log, relu, pow) never broadcast, so they add directly.
---
Shapes tell you the matmul rule: with `C = A @ B`, `A.grad += out.grad @ B.data.T` and `B.grad += A.data.T @ out.grad` are the only arrangements whose shapes work. For `sum`: `g = out.grad; if axis is not None and not keepdims: g = np.expand_dims(g, axis)`, then `self.grad += np.broadcast_to(g, self.shape)`. For `mean`: `s = self.sum(axis, keepdims)` then `s * (s.data.size / self.data.size)`; the backward comes for free.
---
`reshape` backward is `out.grad.reshape(self.shape)`. `transpose`: compute `perm = axes or tuple(range(ndim))[::-1]`, `inverse = np.argsort(perm)`, and in backward `self.grad += out.grad.transpose(inverse)`. `backward()` is the Room 2.1 topological sort with `self.grad = np.ones_like(self.data)` as the seed. Accept `t.reshape((3, 4))` as well as `t.reshape(3, 4)` with `if len(shape) == 1 and isinstance(shape[0], (tuple, list)): shape = tuple(shape[0])`.
