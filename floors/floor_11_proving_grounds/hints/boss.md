Every phase is the same idea: put a second number next to the one being gamed. Phase 1 reuses room 1 (`confusion_matrix`, `accuracy`, `balanced_accuracy`, `macro_f1`); per-class recall is `cm[c, c] / cm[c].sum()`, and the verdict contains the word "lying" only when `accuracy - balanced_accuracy > LYING_GAP`. Phase 2: the length-biased judge cannot prefer the longer answer if both answers have the same number of words, so cut both to `min(len(a.split()), len(b.split()))` words and hand them to room 3's `pairwise_judge_debiased`. Phase 3: score the golden and held-out lists with two `EvalSuite`s and subtract.
---
`filler_ratio`: `words = re.findall(r"[a-z']+", text.lower())`, return `sum(w in FILLER_WORDS for w in words) / len(words)` or 0.0 for no words. `truncate_to_words`: `" ".join(text.split()[:n])`. `split_golden_heldout`: group case indices by `case.tags[0]`, and for each group with at least two members take `k = round(fraction * size)` clamped to `[1, size - 1]`, pick `rng.permutation(size)[:k]` of them for held-out; build both output lists by filtering the original `cases` so order is preserved. Iterate the groups in sorted tag order so the rng is consumed deterministically.
---
`contamination_check` returns `{"golden_score": g, "heldout_score": h, "gap": g - h, "suspicious": g - h > threshold}`. `judging_report` is a dict of `pairwise_judge_naive(...)`, `length_controlled_judge(...)` and the two filler ratios. `GorgonReport.gamed_metrics` appends `"accuracy"`, `"judge_preference"` and `"golden_score"` in that order when their conditions hold (gap > LYING_GAP; naive != controlled; suspicious). The prophecy: the majority classifier games accuracy, the padded answerer wins under the length_biased_judge, the memoriser's golden_score is the flattering one.
