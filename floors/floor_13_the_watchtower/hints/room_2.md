Every metric here is "reference fractions versus current fractions". Get the binning right first: `histogram_bins` is `np.concatenate([[-np.inf], np.quantile(ref, np.linspace(0, 1, n_bins + 1)[1:-1]), [np.inf]])`, and `bin_fractions` is `np.bincount(np.searchsorted(edges[1:-1], x, side="right"), minlength=len(edges) - 1) / len(x)`. PSI is then three lines: clip both fraction vectors with `np.clip(f, eps, None)` and return `float(np.sum((c - r) * np.log(c / r)))`.
---
KS: sort both samples, build `grid = np.concatenate([a, b])`, and compute each CDF on the grid with `np.searchsorted(sorted_sample, grid, side="right") / len(sample)`; the statistic is `np.max(np.abs(f_a - f_b))`. JS: normalise `p` and `q`, form `m = 0.5 * (p + q)`, and write a small `kl(x, y)` that sums `x * log(x / y)` only where `x > 0`; JS is `0.5 * kl(p, m) + 0.5 * kl(q, m)`. Embedding drift: centroids are `ref.mean(axis=0)` and `cur.mean(axis=0)`; cosine is their dot product over the product of norms; clamp `1 - cos` at 0; mean norms come from `np.linalg.norm(x, axis=1).mean()`.
---
`DriftMonitor.observe`: coerce the batch to 2-D (`x[:, None]` for a 1-D batch), check it has `self.n_features` columns, `self._recent.append(x)`, then `current = np.concatenate(list(self._recent))`. Per feature `j`: `psi(self.reference[:, j], current[:, j], self.edges[j])` and `ks_statistic(self.reference[:, j], current[:, j])`. A feature drifted when either exceeds `self.thresholds["psi"]` / `["ks"]`. Build the report dict with `n_current`, the two lists, their maxima, `drifted_features` and `drifted = bool(drifted_features)`; append it to `self.history` and return it.
