In `__init__`: keep `base`, freeze its parameters, then create `self.lora_A = nn.Parameter(torch.empty(r, base.in_features))` initialised with `nn.init.kaiming_uniform_(self.lora_A, a=math.sqrt(5))`, and `self.lora_B = nn.Parameter(torch.zeros(base.out_features, r))`. `self.scaling = alpha / r`, `self.enabled = True`, `self.dropout = nn.Dropout(dropout) if dropout > 0 else nn.Identity()`.
---
`forward`: `out = self.base(x)`; if not enabled return it; otherwise `return out + (self.dropout(x) @ self.lora_A.T @ self.lora_B.T) * self.scaling`. Shapes: `x (..., in) @ A.T (in, r) -> (..., r) @ B.T (r, out) -> (..., out)`. `delta_weight` is `(self.lora_B @ self.lora_A) * self.scaling`, shape `(out, in)`. `merge` builds `nn.Linear(in, out, bias=base.bias is not None)` and, under `torch.no_grad()`, copies `base.weight + delta_weight()` into its weight and `base.bias` into its bias.
---
`inject_lora`: `for parent_name, parent in list(model.named_modules()): for child_name, child in list(parent.named_children()): if isinstance(child, nn.Linear) and child_name in targets: setattr(parent, child_name, LoRALinear(child, r, alpha, dropout))` and record `f"{parent_name}.{child_name}"`. `lora_modules` filters `named_modules()` by `isinstance(m, LoRALinear)`. `lora_state_dict` filters `model.state_dict()` by keys ending in `lora_A`/`lora_B` (clone the values). `lora_parameter_count` sums `m.lora_A.numel() + m.lora_B.numel()` over `lora_modules`.
