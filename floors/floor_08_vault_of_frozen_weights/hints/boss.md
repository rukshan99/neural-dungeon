Phase 1 is Room 4's `lora_finetune_copy` with Room 4's replay mixing folded in: deepcopy, freeze all, `inject_lora(r=8, alpha=16)`, AdamW at lr 3e-3 over the trainable params, and every batch is `torch.cat` of 8 ledger rows and 8 chronicles rows (two `make_batches` streams). Return the copy in eval mode. LoRA alone leaves the chronicles loss ~1.3 higher; the replay rows are what keep it within 0.35.
---
Phase 2: `save_adapter` reads the first `LoRALinear` for `r` and `alpha`, collects the attribute names (`name.rsplit(".", 1)[-1]`) as `targets`, and stores `lora_state_dict(model)` under `"weights"`. `load_adapter`: if `lora_modules(model)` is empty, freeze and `inject_lora` with the saved config, then `dict(model.named_parameters())[key].copy_(value)` under `torch.no_grad()`. `disable_adapters`/`enable_adapters` set `m.enabled` on every `lora_modules` entry.
---
Phase 3: `exported = copy.deepcopy(model)`; walk `named_modules()`/`named_children()` exactly as `inject_lora` does but replace each `LoRALinear` child with `child.merge()`; return `{k: v.detach().clone() for k, v in exported.state_dict().items()}`. Phase 4: a strategy survives only if it learns the ledger (<= 1.6) AND its chronicles loss rises <= 0.35. Only the two replay strategies do. Full fine-tune and LoRA-alone both learn the ledger and both forget.
