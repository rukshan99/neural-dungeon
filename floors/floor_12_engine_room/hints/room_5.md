Everything goes through one generator: `greedy_token_stream(model, idx, n)` yields an `int` per step (`logits, _ = model(idx)`, argmax of the last position, `torch.cat`, `yield int(next_id)`). `/generate` collects the whole stream and decodes it; `/stream` writes one line per yielded token. `validate_request` is a pure function: `json.loads` inside a `try` that catches `json.JSONDecodeError` and `UnicodeDecodeError`, then check the types one by one and return `(None, message)` on the first problem.
---
`start()`: `self._httpd = ThreadingHTTPServer((self.host, self.port), make_handler(self))`; `self.port = self._httpd.server_address[1]`; `self._thread = threading.Thread(target=self._httpd.serve_forever, daemon=True)`; `self._thread.start()`. `stop()`: `self._httpd.shutdown()`, `self._httpd.server_close()`, `self._thread.join()`. In the handler, read the body with `self.rfile.read(int(self.headers.get("Content-Length") or 0))`, and reply with `send_response(status)`, `send_header("Content-Type", "application/json")`, `send_header("Content-Length", str(len(body)))`, `end_headers()`, `self.wfile.write(body)`. Override `log_message(self, *args): pass`.
---
Streaming: `send_response(200)`, `send_header("Content-Type", "application/x-ndjson")`, `send_header("Connection", "close")`, `end_headers()`, then for every token `self.wfile.write((json.dumps({"token": tokenizer.decode([tok])}) + "\n").encode()); self.wfile.flush()`, and finally `b'{"done": true}\n'`. No Content-Length: with HTTP/1.0 the body ends when the connection closes, which the handler does for you after `do_POST` returns. Wrap the model call in `with self._lock:` so two petitioners are served one after the other.
